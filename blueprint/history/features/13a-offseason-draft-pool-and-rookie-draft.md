# Feature: Offseason: draft pool and rookie draft

**From build-plan:** feature 13a
**Build attempt:** 1
**Status:** verified
**Branch:** `feature/offseason-draft-pool-and-rookie-draft`

## Goal

Add the rookie draft as a new wizard step after the free agency draft. Once free agency is done, the app works out how many position players and goaltenders the participating teams still need to reach 6 and 2, generates a draft pool of exactly that many plus two extra position players and one extra goaltender (draft charts, draft ages 18-22, random last names), and the commissioner picks for each team in reverse finishing order. The pool and the picks are saved in the league's staged offseason plan. Live teams, rosters and the name registry stay untouched until feature 13c approves the offseason.

This is the first of three pieces split from feature 13 (13a draft pool and rookie draft, 13b progression and retiree replacement, 13c approval and next season).

## Decisions (from the user and the program specs)

- **Pool size:** per participating team, need = 6 minus its position players and 2 minus its goaltenders after retirement, release and its free agency picks (never below 0; a surplus on one team does not offset a shortage on another). Pool = total position-player need + 2, and total goaltender need + 1. New teams never take part.
- **Generation:** draftees use the draft charts that already exist (`generateDraftStandardPlayer`, `generateDraftGoaltender`): age 18-22, OF+DE total from the draft chart, split, control stars and sportsmanship as in initial generation, goaltending from the draft goaltending chart. Names are random last names that are not burned, not on any stored roster, and not on any staged new team or draftee in any saved plan. Undrafted draftees are not burned and are destroyed at approval (13c).
- **Order (user rule recorded in 12b):** the exact reverse of the free agency order: promoted arrivals first (worst finish first among them), then staying teams worst finish to best, then demoted arrivals last (worst finish first among them). Derived from `draftOrder(...)` reversed, never stored.
- **Rounds:** each team picks until its roster is full. The team on the clock is the first team in draft order, among teams that still have a need, with the fewest rookie picks so far (so teams pick in rounds, and a team that is full is skipped).
- **Kind of pick (my default; confirm in review):** a team may only pick a kind it still needs. With the pool sized as above this means the draft can always finish and no team ends over the limit because of the rookie draft. A team over the limit after free agency (12b flags it) simply has no need and is skipped.
- **The commissioner makes every pick.** "Undo last pick" removes the most recent pick. The pool can be regenerated only while there are no picks (undo them first), so a regenerate never silently discards picks.
- **Stale results:** changing a protection, re-running aging, making or undoing a free agency pick, saving departures or restaging arrivals clears the draft pool and the rookie picks, like it clears free agency picks. A free agency pick changes who needs what, so the pool must be regenerated afterwards.

## In scope

- `draftPool` and `draftPicks` on `OffseasonPlan` with validation.
- Pure helpers: team needs, pool size, rookie draft order, team on the clock, pick validation.
- Draft-player builders in `roster.ts` (standard and goaltender) mirroring `generateStandard` and `generateGoalie`.
- Repository operations: generate (or regenerate) the pool, make a pick, undo the last pick; clearing the pool and picks when their inputs change.
- (Added at the user's request after the build) A collapsed-by-default "Show roster" panel for the team on the clock, in both the free agency and rookie draft steps (`src/components/offseason/team-roster-panel.tsx`): the team's players after retirement and release plus its free agency and rookie picks, with protected and pick markers.
- A `rookie-draft` wizard step with not-ready, pool-not-generated, on-the-clock and complete states, dice shown next to each draftee.

## Out of scope

- Moving drafted players onto live rosters, ageing, retiring, burning names, discarding undrafted draftees, creating the next season, approval (13c).
- Progression rolls and replacing retirees with new random draftees (13b).
- Offseason screens for leagues without relegation (13c).
- Letting teams pass, trading, picking a kind a team does not need, or editing a draftee (including its name).

## Data / contracts

- No new collection. `offseason-plans` documents gain optional `draftPool` (full player records, ids from `createId`, ages 18-22) and `draftPicks` (`{ franchiseId, playerId }` in pick order); an old document without them stays valid, so no migration or version bump.
- The pool is stored (not derived) because it is random; the order, needs and team on the clock are derived from the current archive, plan and linked-league archives, so they cannot disagree with them.
- Consumer contract for 13b and 13c: a drafted player joins the picking team's roster when the offseason is applied (drafted players are burned then; undrafted draftees are destroyed and their names stay free); the stored draftee ages are final draft ages (no `AGING_YEARS` for draftees); 13b may append more random draftees for progression retirees outside this pool; 13c applies `RosterPlayer` ages below 20, so its roster validation must allow 18-22 for new players.
- Writes: one `put` of this league's plan per operation, through `acceptQueue`; reads fail closed; no franchise, roster, season, retired-player or registry document is written.

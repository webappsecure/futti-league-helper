Fütti League and Game Tracker

Fütti (pronounced “footy”) is a made-up sport closely modeled on futsal, a real-world indoor version of soccer that young players often use to sharpen their skills. Instead of eleven players on a big field, each Fütti team puts five on a small pitch: a goalkeeper, two attackers and two defenders, plus a few substitutes on the bench. A match lasts forty minutes, split into two halves. The clock stops for fouls, injuries, goals and out-of-bounds balls, and there’s no extra time added at the end. Fütti changes a few of futsal’s rules to give it its own character. Fouls escalate from a simple warning, to a yellow card that sends a player to the sidelines until the next major stoppage, to a red card that removes them for the rest of the game and gives the other team a penalty kick. Games can also end early if one team builds a big enough lead late in the match, as if the leaders were running out the clock. Because the sport isn’t real, there are no real leagues, teams or star players to follow. The fun is in inventing your own.

As a tabletop game, Fütti lets one person act out a full match on a printed game board using ordinary dice and paper player cards. Each card shows a player rated with a few numbers: how good they are at attacking, defending and keeping their temper, plus a goalkeeping rating for keepers. The game comes with a set of unnamed generic players, so you can draft or randomly deal them into your own teams and leagues. Play moves one game minute at a time. A roll of two dice picks which attacker and defender face off that minute. A second roll compares each player’s rating to the dice to decide whether the attacker gets a shot, the defender steals the ball, or the ball comes loose. Shots are then rolled against the goalkeeper’s rating. When the dice come up doubles, you check a special chart for dramatic events like hard tackles, scuffles, injuries, yellow and red cards, and breakaways. As you keep score on a scoresheet, the dice gradually tell the story of a believable match, so the game feels like coaching and watching a sports broadcast unfold at the same time.

I want to create a program that is targetted to use on a tablet (either Android or iPad) but can also be used on a Windows or Mac computer or laptop as well. The technology of choice will be React Native Expo. 

This program will be able to create Fütti leagues, manage the league throughout a season, and manage the offseason as well. In addition, the program will be able to support scheduling a season, playing each game, and keeping stats for all of the players and goaltenders in the league. Because most Fütti leagues are promotion and relegation leagues, there should be support for that as part of the offseason process as well.

Here is a rough layout of how the progression of development should flow:

1. Basic League Creation
- Choose between a 6 team league, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, and 20
- The first version of this tool will not support divisions and conferences, but make sure the program is designed in a way that support can be added easily in the future.
- Choose between a relegation league or non relegation league.
- If the league is a relegation league choose how many teams get promoted and how many teams get promoted each season. These are different values. It is possible to have one league have two teams getting demoted while none get promoted, while the other league has two getting promoted and none getting demoted, representing a senior league and junior league where the promoted teams from the junior league go to the senior league the next season and the two teams from the senior league getting demoted go to the junior league the next season.
- Choose between the team name being used (like AFC Richmond) or city and team name (like Richmond Greyhounds)
- Each team will have a city, a name, and primary and secondary colors chosen by a color picker.
- Each team will have an intial team grade from A, B, C, D, and F - A being the best and F being the worst.
- Leagues can be added, edited, and deleted. If deleting a league, confirm deletion first.

4. Run a league offseason based on the league creation guide.
	- if the league is a relegation league, choose teams leaving the league through promotion and relegation and bring teams into the league to replace them. These can either be created teams or teams from another league, or teams that have previously left the league you are playing currently. 	- If new teams are being created to replace demoted teams, determine the city, name and colors for the new team. They also get an intial team grade based on a roll of 1-6. On a 1, the team is rated a C. On a 2-3 the team is rated a D. On a 4-6 the team is rated an F.
	- If new teams are being created to replace promoted teams, determine the city, name and colors for the new team. They also get an intial team grade based on a roll of 1-6. On a 1, the team is rated an A. On a 2-3 the team is rated a B. On a 4-6 the team is rated an C.
	- New teams get a random team of players based on their intial team grade. They do not participate in the draft or free agency. Their initial team is generated per the charts from initial team creation.
	- Existing teams in other leagues can be brought in as well. Players for teams replacing a demoted team will be be reduced by one point in offense and defense (minimum of one). Regenerate each player's control stars based on the control star section in initial player generation. Goaltenders will be reduced two points (minimum 31 see notes in team creation on how to properly reduce a goaltender value). These teams WILL participate in free agency and draft, aging and offseason player changes.
	- Players for teams replacing a promoted team will be be increated by one point in offense and defense (maximum of five, and no players with more than eight total skill between offense and defense). Regenerate each player's control stars based on the control star section in initial player generation. Goaltenders will be increased two points (maximum 46 see notes in team creation on how to properly increate a goaltender value).
